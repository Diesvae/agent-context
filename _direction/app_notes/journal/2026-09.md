# Journal — Notes, 2026-09

## 2026-09-17 — the branch is created as the worked example

Notes exists so the rules have something real to be checked against. Two domains and one
contract, deliberately: one domain would not exercise the module boundary, and no contract
would leave the cross-app partitioning assertion untested.

`open_store()` was added after `NoteStore` rather than with it. Reason recorded because it is
the kind of thing that looks arbitrary later: a store handed back un-indexed answers `slugs()`
with an empty list, so an empty catalog and a missing directory became indistinguishable to
the caller — and one of those is a bug while the other is legitimate state.

## 2026-09-17 — search index left open rather than decided

Measured on the 41-note local corpus: a naive scan of every body answers in about 6 ms, and
the corpus grows by roughly 3 notes a week. At that scale all three candidate index shapes are
indistinguishable, so any argument from speed today is an argument from the one regime where
the measurement cannot discriminate. Recorded as `app_notes #1` rather than settled in
passing.
