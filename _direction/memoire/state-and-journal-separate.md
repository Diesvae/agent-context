name: state-and-journal-separate
description: Triage has more destinations than it looks; the fourth is the one that vanishes.
metadata:
  type: feedback
---

A piece of work produces four natures of text, and they belong in four places: the rule now in
force goes to the **state** fiche, the measurement that justified it goes to the **journal**,
the transferable lesson goes to **memory**, and what was **not settled** goes to the open
register. The fourth is the one that disappears, because it does not present itself as an
acquisition — nobody feels they have learned something by failing to decide.

**Why:** mixed into one paragraph, these four grow without bound, and the invariant at the top
of a fiche ends up contradicted by prose that accumulated underneath it. Separated at the
moment of writing, each stays the size its purpose needs. A state fiche can then be read whole
at boot, which is the only reason it is readable at all.

**How to apply:** at the end of a piece of work, ask the four questions in order and write each
answer to its own home before moving on. An entry with nothing to put in the first line is not
a direction decision — it is a session report, and it belongs in the journal alone. See
[[a-green-guard-may-be-dead]] for what happens when the measurement is skipped.
